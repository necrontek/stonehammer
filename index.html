Star Player Points (SPP)

Standard Blood Bowl SPP awards are used for this short-term Sevens league. Players earn SPP during league matches and can spend them on skills / improvements between games.

Action SPP Awarded
Touchdown 3
Casualty (inflicted) 2
Interception 2
Completion (accurate pass caught) 1
Most Valuable Player (MVP) 5

Typical advancement thresholds (approximate – confirm with your league rules):
Level 1 → 2: 6 SPP  |  Level 2 → 3: 12 SPP  |  Level 3 → 4: 18 SPP, etc.
